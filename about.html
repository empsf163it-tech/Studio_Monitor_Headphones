OUR PURPOSE

Audio gear should be easier to understand.

SonoVis is a concept guide for people exploring studio monitor headphones for music production, recording, podcasting, and focused listening.

Our goal is to explain common features in plain language, organize options around real use cases, and encourage thoughtful buying decisions—not to make every product sound right for everyone.

HOW WE EVALUATE

Editorial standards & methodology

STEP 01

Acoustic Verification

We analyze manufacturer impedance curves, driver size specs, and isolation dB performance across real home studio environments.

STEP 02

Workflow Suitability

We categorize models strictly by practical use case — vocal tracking, podcast editing, beatmaking, or critical mastering.

STEP 03

Long-term Durability

We assess cable stress relief, headband hinge metal structure, and earpad replacement availability over long-term use.
